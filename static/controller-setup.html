Controller Overlay

Show your Xbox or PS5 controller on stream, with click sounds. Pick your options, then copy the URL into an OBS Browser Source. Back to the heart-rate dashboard

In OBS, tick "Control audio via OBS" on the source so the clicks show in the audio mixer.
Press a button on your controller to see it here. Browsers only expose a controller after a button press.
  1. In OBS add a Browser Source and paste the URL.
  2. Set Width and Height to 800 × 520.
  3. The controller must be connected to the same PC that runs OBS.
